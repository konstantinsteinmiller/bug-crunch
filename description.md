## How to play

**Stop the bugs before the clock runs out.** Every level asks for a number of
squishes; the bar under your score shows how close you are.

1. **Move the shoe.** It follows your finger (or your mouse). The dashed ring on
   the floor is exactly what the next stomp will hit — nothing outside it dies.
2. **Squish.** A tap is a quick stomp. Everything inside the ring goes splat.
3. **Chain them.** Squish again within a second and a half and the multiplier
   climbs: ×2, ×3, ×5, ×8 … up to ×50. The ring around the chain badge is the
   window draining — beat it and the chain keeps going.
4. **Hold for a SLAM.** Some bugs have shells a tap bounces off (you will hear
   the CLANG). Press and hold to charge, then let go: a slam hits harder, reaches
   further, and cracks armour a tap cannot.
5. **Do not stomp the spiky ones.** The caterpillar hurts. Herd it, wait it out,
   or buy the steel boot.
6. **Fill the vial, then hit FEVER.** Every squish adds juice, and a chain adds
   more. When the vial on the left is full the flame button lights up: tap it for
   ten seconds of the giant golden boot, where nothing is armoured and nothing is
   safe.
7. **Three stars a level.** One for clearing it, two more for the level's own
   challenges — a chain to reach, a time to beat, an accuracy to hold. Stars open
   new worlds and unlock better shoes in the Locker.

Every tenth level is a **boss**: a huge bug with three phases and a health bar.
Watch the call-out above the bar — it tells you what it is about to do.

## Controls

**Phone / tablet**

| Gesture | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drag | Move the shoe. It rides slightly above your finger so your thumb never covers the target. |
| Tap | Quick stomp. |
| Press and hold, then release | Heavy slam — bigger circle, cracks armour. |
| Double tap in the same spot | Heel pivot: a quick sweep around the foot. |
| Tap the flame button (left) | Splat Fever, when the vial is full. |

**Mouse and keyboard**

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Move the mouse | Move the shoe. |
| Left click | Quick stomp. |
| Hold left click, then release | Heavy slam. |
| Right click | Instant slam, at full power. |
| Double click in the same spot | Heel pivot. |
| `Space` | Splat Fever, when the vial is full. |
| `Esc` | Settings. |

**Accessibility**

* **High-visibility ring** — a solid, heavier stomp circle instead of the dashed
  one.
* **Single-tap mode** — the foot snaps to the nearest bug when you tap, for
  players who cannot aim and press at the same time.
* **Calm mode** — screen shake and flashes off.
* **Juice style** — Cartoon Ooze (default), Confetti & Candy, or Bubble Pop, for
  players who would rather the bugs popped into sweets than into goo.

All of the above are in Settings, and every one of them is saved with your
progress.
